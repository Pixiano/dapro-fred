What is data science?
Data science is the study of data, much like marine biology is the study of sea-dwelling biological life forms.
Data scientists
construct questions around specific data sets and then use
data analytics
and
advanced analytics
to find patterns, create predictive models, and develop insights that guide decision-making within businesses.
