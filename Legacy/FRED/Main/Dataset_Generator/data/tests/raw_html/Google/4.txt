Here's how you can update Chrome
Updates automatically
Chrome checks for new updates regularly, and when an update is available, Chrome applies it automatically when you close and reopen the browser.
Get more
help
Applying a pending update
If you haven't closed your browser in a while, you might see a pending update in the top-right corner of your browser, next to your profile picture.
Chrome will automatically update the next time that you close and reopen Chrome. But if you want to apply the update straight away, just click the three dots to the right of the message and select the first menu item.
