## clock/smart_turn.py
Smart Turn v3.2 int8 ONNX (pipecat, BSD-2, 8 M parameters, CPU): probability that the speaker's turn is complete, from the raw audio (last 8 s, zero-padded at the front), Whisper-style log-mel via faster-whisper's feature extractor (no `transformers`). ~41 ms per call on one thread here. Found while wiring: the output named "logits" is already a probability (a second sigmoid squashed everything to 0.73). Quick sanity on Kokoro-spoken sentences: complete sentence + 0.3 s silence 0.98-0.99; cuts at arbitrary points mostly low-to-mixed (0.02-0.97, a TTS cut often lands on a word end), so the real accuracy gate is the labelled offline harness (next), on clause-boundary cuts and on Vatsal's own speech. Weights live in `Core/weights/` (git-ignored; recipe in Core/README).

## clock/voice_id.py + tests/voice_eval.py
ECAPA-TDNN speaker gate (CPU, ~80 ms), cosine against stored references; `score()` is `None` ("unknown", never "no" and never "yes") when nothing is enrolled or the clip is shorter than `voice_min_s`. References stored outside the repo. **Nobody is enrolled** (Vatsal asked not to train it yet; an enrollment I made from the old clips was deleted).
Evaluation on the new studio set (`Projects/Claude/Project_COPY/Data/Vatsal_v1`: 516 segments of 3-14 s at 24 kHz from 8 long recordings, speech-enhanced; read in place, numbers only):
- Same speaker, different recording (enrolled on 6 sources, tested on 2 held-out sources, 34 segments): similarity p5 0.66, median 0.75, min 0.63. Synthetic other voices: max 0.27. Clean separation.
- Length matters: first 0.6 s median 0.40; 1 s 0.50; 2 s 0.62; 4 s 0.71 (others stay under 0.27). At threshold 0.30: 0% reject at 1 s and longer, 8.8% at 0.6 s.
- **Domain gap**: the same enrollment against Vatsal's real live-mic "Hey FRED" 1-s clips scores median 0.21 (p95 0.35): the enhanced studio audio does not transfer to the live mic path. Enrolling from this set alone would reject him live (90% at 0.30). Enrollment therefore has to include live-mic speech, which is why utterance collection is now on.
- Settings now: `voice_threshold` 0.30 (provisional), `voice_min_s` 1.0, `voice_refs_cap` 20. Retune once live-mic utterances are collected; real strangers will sit closer to him than the synthetic voices used as imposters, so the false-accept side is a floor, not a measurement.

## clock/turn_controller.py
One tick every 160 ms (driven by the mic chunk timestamps, so a replay is deterministic), one output state on the bus (`clock.state`): LISTEN, END_OF_TURN, BARGE_IN, BACKCHANNEL, IGNORE. Rules as planned, every threshold a setting: end of turn = VAD silence >= 200 ms AND Smart Turn >= 0.5, or 1.2 s hard cap, or chord release (while the chord is held only release ends it), or `max_utterance_s`; a turn nobody addressed (no hotkey, no wake word in the 8 s before/during, not inside the 8 s follow-up window after FRED spoke) ends as IGNORE `not_addressed`; voice ID False ends as IGNORE `not_vatsal` (skipped for hotkey turns; None = unknown = pass, so nothing is rejected before anyone is enrolled); barge-in = voiced speech >= 300 ms while FRED speaks AND voice not False AND (headphones OR mic RMS >= `barge_level`), emitted once, the utterance then carries on as a normal turn; shorter speech while FRED talks is a BACKCHANNEL (nothing cancelled). A failing Smart Turn model is logged (`clock.error`) and the silence rules still end the turn. 11 scripted scenarios in the self-check (wake before/during speech, resume after a pause, hotkey hold, barge-in/backchannel, speakers vs headphones, follow-up window, model failure).
Echo handling today = the level gate; the real echo floor (speakers) is measured in P4 when FRED actually speaks.

## Offline gate: `python tests/offline_clock.py` (Kokoro, 4 voices x 5 sentences, real Mic -> VAD -> Capture -> clock path, a room noise floor in every silence)
- Complete sentence, wait from the last word to END_OF_TURN: rules A (VAD silence only) and B (+ Smart Turn) both median 0.36 s, p90 0.56-0.60 s; rule C (hard cap only) median 1.36 s.
- **False cutoffs inside a sentence** (pause of 0.4 / 0.7 / 1.0 s): A and B 85-100 % of cases; only C (1.2 s hard cap) never cuts (0 %). **Smart Turn adds nothing here**: B equals A within one case.
- Why, checked: the model's input features were verified identical to the reference implementation (max difference 1.7e-6 against transformers' WhisperFeatureExtractor, same probability), so the pipeline is right. On Kokoro speech cut at a word gap, the model says "complete" (0.8-0.99) even after "Set a timer for"; digital silence pushes it up further (0.63 -> 0.98 inside 0.2 s of exact zeros), so the harness uses a noise floor. On Vatsal's real studio narration (the Vatsal_v1 segments, 60 sentence ends and 180 internal word gaps, enhanced audio): at 0.5 it accepts 58 % of real sentence ends and wrongly accepts 40 % of internal gaps; at 0.9 it accepts 27 % of ends and 14 % of gaps. So on these inputs it is a weak signal. Caveats: narration, enhanced audio, TTS are not live conversational commands, and internal energy minima include genuine phrase ends.
- While FRED speaks: a 0.15 s interjection is a BACKCHANNEL 5/5, 0.20 s 2/5, 0.25 s 0/5, a 1 s interruption a BARGE_IN 5/5; the VAD smears speech by about 90 ms, so the 300 ms rule effectively separates at about 210 ms of real speech (`barge_ms` is the knob). No wake word / hotkey: IGNORE not_addressed 5/5.
- Decision pending (Vatsal): keep the planned rule B and judge it on live-mic data (collection is on), or lean on silence length (hard cap) / hotkey for long thinking pauses until the data exists.
