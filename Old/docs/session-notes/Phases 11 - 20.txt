📘 FRED v2.0 — Phases 11–20

(a.k.a. “The Awakening Protocol”)
Total estimate: ~2 months (speedrun ~4–5 weeks)
Target: Semi-agentic, multi-modal, self-improving FRED


Phase 11 – Central Classifier Integration

⏱ ~1 week
Tagline: “The Dispatcher Awakens”
Goal: Unify all FRED modules under one “brainstem” to detect intent.
Tasks:

Primary classifier distinguishes OS commands vs. general chat/queries.
Routes input to the correct handler (OS executor, LLM core, or other modules).
Acts as a traffic controller for every incoming query.
Outcome: FRED can instantly understand “who should think next.”

Phase 12 – Web Search & API Expansion

⏱ ~1 week
Tagline: “Hands Beyond the LAN”
Goal: Give FRED access to live information and external services.
Tasks:

websearch_handler() for live info (DuckDuckGo/SerpAPI).
Modular api_agent() for apps like weather, Swiggy, Amazon, etc.
Triggered only when classifier detects external knowledge intent.
Outcome: FRED gains “live awareness” and starts acting beyond the PC.

Phase 13 – Presentation & School Demo

⏱ ~1 week
Tagline: “First Light in Public”
Goal: Showcase FRED in a school or LAN environment.
Tasks:

Scripted demo of OS control, chat, and web/API handling.
Visual UI displays module activation.
Voice responses enabled for interactivity.
Outcome: FRED performs smoothly, demonstrating reflexes, intelligence, and personality.

Phase 14 – Fine-Tuning & Core Refinement

⏱ 1–2 weeks
Tagline: “The Neural Growth”
Goal: Teach FRED your personal style and task efficiency.
Tasks:

Collect conversation + task logs for fine-tuning.
Reinforce reasoning, personality, and context retention.
Offline QLoRA pipeline for efficient updates.
Outcome: FRED becomes Vatsal-tuned, learning to think and respond in your style.

Phase 15 – Hardware Expansion (RTX 5060Ti Era)

⏱ 1 week
Tagline: “Muscles for the Mind”
Goal: Exploit new GPU power for heavier computations.
Tasks:

GPU acceleration for local models (PyTorch + CUDA).
Hardware detection, benchmarking, and monitoring module.
Prepares FRED for future submodules: voice cloning, real-time vision.
Outcome: FRED now knows and leverages its own computational body.

Phase 16 – Task Queue & % Autonomy

⏱ 1–2 weeks
Tagline: “The Semi-Autonomous Mind”
Goal: Enable multi-tasking with controlled autonomy.
Tasks:

Task queue system for sequential or parallel execution.
Internal state tracks pending, active, and completed tasks.
Configurable autonomy percentage for independent task handling.
Outcome: FRED moves from reactive to semi-autonomous — it can work while you do.

Phase 17 – Emotional Intelligence

⏱ 1 week
Tagline: “Feeling in the Wires”
Goal: Add adaptive emotional responses.
Tasks:

Fine-tuning with emotional datasets (empathetic dialogues, tone adaptation).
Sentiment analysis → emotion tagging → tone modulation (text + TTS).
Store short-term emotional context for influencing replies.
Outcome: FRED can respond contextually with emotional nuance, enhancing realism.

Phase 18 – Dynamic Memory Fusion

⏱ 1 week
Tagline: “The Mind Remembers”
Goal: Merge short-term and long-term memory into an episodic structure.
Tasks:

Vectorize memory entries for similarity search.
Auto-forgetting for irrelevant info.
Supports emotional and task-context recall.
Outcome: FRED remembers past interactions intelligently, improving context awareness.

Phase 19 – Visual Core: “Orange Mind” UI

⏱ 1 week
Tagline: “A Face for the Mind”
Goal: Give FRED a modern, Jarvis-style visual identity.
Tasks:

Dynamic orange energy blob representing active thought.
Visual feedback for tasks, queue, and module activations.
UI built with HTML/CSS + WebSocket, responsive and interactive.
Outcome: FRED becomes visually alive — a signature identity that matches its intelligence.

Phase 20 – Autonomous Experimentation

⏱ 2 weeks
Tagline: “The Mind Upgrades Itself”
Goal: Enable FRED to perform safe autonomous improvements.
Tasks:

Task queue + emotional + fine-tuning layers combine for emergent behavior.
Sandbox testing: FRED can create datasets, evaluate accuracy, or optimize modules.
Optional “Reflection” stage: evaluates task outcomes and improves next iteration.
Outcome: FRED achieves semi-autonomous operation — your personal AI that learns to upgrade itself.

💡 Phase 11–20 Summary:

FRED evolves from reactive assistant → semi-agentic AI with reflexes, live awareness, and emergent personality.

Architecture is modular, hierarchical, and extensible — perfect foundation for future KojiGPT without mixing them yet.

GPU, task queue, emotional intelligence, and visual identity complete the “god-tier 15-year-old AI” build.
