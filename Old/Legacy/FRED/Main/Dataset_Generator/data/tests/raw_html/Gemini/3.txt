What is personalized help from Gemini and how does it work?
Gemini can now provide even more relevant and personalized responses based on what it knows about you.
It works by analyzing your prompt and determining if your saved info, past chats, or more could be helpful in formulating a response - ensuring the information that you receive is tailored to your interests and needs.
Where is personalized help from Gemini available?
Personalized help from Gemini is available on the web and mobile. This experience is not yet available to under 18s, Google Workspace, or Education users.
