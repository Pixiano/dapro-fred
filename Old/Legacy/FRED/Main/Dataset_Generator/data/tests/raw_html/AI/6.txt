Our approach to Responsible AI
Given its risks and complexities, we believe that we as a company must pursue AI responsibly. As leaders in AI, we must lead not only in state-of-the-art AI technologies, but also in state-of-the-art responsible AIâits innovation and implementation. In 2018, we were one of the first companies to articulate
AI Principles

Our responsible AI approach includes the following:
Focus on AI that is useful and benefits users and society.

Intentionally apply our
AI Principles

Apply the scientific method to AI R&D

Set benchmarks and measure performance and progress on different factors of responsible AI. Create innovative tools (e.g. for safety) to keep pace with AI technologies.
